Our Community

In 2006, CEO Mr. Mohamed Abou Gelail established the Sakaran Society Association for Development — built to care for the society and small farmers of Fayoum. By 2010, under his leadership as Board Chair, the association became the first Fairtrade-certified association for small farmers in Egypt. Through Fairtrade, farmers' incomes were transformed — selling at prices higher than market, with promotional projects for development. What started with 12 members now encompasses over 196 members and 313 hectares.

The Association

Sakaran Society
for Development

The association was built with a singular purpose: to care for the community and the small farmers who form the backbone of Egypt's herb industry. By achieving Fairtrade certification — a first for Egypt — the association unlocked a new economic reality for its members. Farmers no longer needed to accept market prices. They had collective power, institutional support, and a direct pipeline to international buyers who value ethical trade.

Although Mr. Abou Gelail stepped down from the board in 2017, Safe Herbs & Spices continues to support the association with full consultation services, and purchases more than 85% of association production at prices far above the local market.
