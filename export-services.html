Common Questions

Export FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for herb and spice exports? +

MOQ varies by product. For most herbs and seeds we start from 500 kg per product. Trial orders from 100 kg are available for new customers. Contact us at info@safeherbsco.com for exact MOQ, pricing, and a free sample.

Do you offer private label or white-label spice packaging? +

Yes. We offer a complete private label packaging service — retail pouches, Doypacks, tins, and bulk sacks — with full EU and UK labelling compliance support. Custom printing is available.

Which certifications cover your export products? +

All exports are covered by EU Organic, USDA NOP, FSSC 22000, BRC Global, ISO 9001:2015, Fairtrade, Rainforest Alliance, Halal, and NFSA. Transaction certificates and COAs are provided with every shipment. Download PDFs from our Certifications page.

Can you supply chamomile wholesale from Fayoum, Egypt? +

Yes. Fayoum is our primary chamomile growing region. We supply EU Organic and USDA NOP certified chamomile in bulk from our own 195-hectare Fayoum farms — the source guarantees traceability and consistency. Contact us for pricing and samples.

What payment terms and shipping options do you offer? +

We work with international standard payment terms including L/C, T/T, and DP. Shipping is by sea freight (FCL/LCL) from Alexandria or Port Said, Egypt. Air freight is available for trial orders and urgent delivery.

Do you export to the EU, USA, and Gulf countries? +

Yes. We regularly export to Europe, the USA, Gulf countries (KSA, UAE, Kuwait), and global markets worldwide. Our certifications (EU Organic, USDA NOP, Halal) are specifically designed to meet the import requirements of each market.
