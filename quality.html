Our Standard

Once raw material arrives at our factory, it is inspected sensorially by the quality team and then analysed by external accredited laboratories. We do not release any material for processing until results comply fully with required specifications. Every step — from gravity separation to final container testing — is documented, sampled and retained for two full years as a traceable quality record.

After initial cleaning, material enters the precision milling and optical colour-sorting lines. State-of-the-art optical sorters detect and reject any foreign bodies or off-colour material at machine speed, ensuring a product of exceptional purity.

Milling to customer-specified particle size
Pre-sort sieving to protect optical sorter from blockage
Optical colour sorting — rejects discoloured material
Final sieve pass to clear any residual foreign bodies

Our Lab Tests

What We Test

Every product batch is tested against international standards before release — no exceptions, no shortcuts.

01
Sensory Analysis
Visual, olfactory and taste inspection by trained specialists on every incoming batch. Colour, texture, aroma and appearance are graded against reference standards.
02
Pesticide Residues
Full multi-residue pesticide screening by accredited external laboratories. Results must meet EU MRL (Maximum Residue Level) limits and organic certification standards.
03
Microbiology
Total plate count, yeast & mould, Salmonella, E. coli and other pathogens — all tested to international food safety limits (FSSC 22000, ISO 22000).
04
Moisture & Ash
Precise moisture content and total ash measurements ensure correct drying and purity levels — critical for shelf life, weight accuracy and customer specification compliance.
05
Foreign Body Detection
Metal detection on every finished product line. Optical sorting catches colour-variant foreign material. Sieve analysis confirms particle size distribution.
06
Traceability Archive
Retained samples from every batch and container are stored for 2 years. Dispatch records link each shipment to its test certificates — full chain-of-custody documentation available on request.
