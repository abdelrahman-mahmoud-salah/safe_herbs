## 2. COMPETITOR GAPS — 5 Missed Keyword Opportunities

These are angles competitors in the Egyptian spice export space consistently underutilize:

| # | Gap Keyword | Rationale |
|---|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | **"EU organic certified cumin Egypt"** | Most Egyptian exporters don't lead with specific certification + product + origin combos. 3-word specificity = low competition, high buyer intent. |
| 2 | **"private label herbs packaging Egypt"** | European brands seeking white-label supply rarely find dedicated content for this from Egyptian exporters. A dedicated page would dominate. |
| 3 | **"bulk chamomile wholesale Fayoum"** | Fayoum is Egypt's chamomile capital. Almost no exporter ranks for the geographic origin + product + volume combo. |
| 4 | **"FSSC 22000 spice supplier"** | Food safety standard queries are purely B2B; almost no Egyptian spice site has a dedicated page for it, yet buyers filter by this. |
| 5 | **"herbs seeds export MOQ"** (Minimum Order Quantity) | Importers consistently search for MOQ + product because it directly qualifies the supplier. No competitor addresses this transparently. |
