Abou Gelail Family

82 Years of
Craftsmanship

Safe Herbs & Spices is the fruit of three generations of the Abou Gelail family — 82 years of craftsmanship and experience in the art of herbs, seeds, and spices farming. From a small family farm in Fayoum, we have grown to become one of Egypt's largest family-owned organic herb companies.

1942
The Foundation
The Abou Gelail family begins cultivating herbs and seeds on the fertile lands of Fayoum — laying the foundation for what will become Egypt's leading organic herb company.
2005
First Organic Certification
Fayoum farms receive their first organic certification — marking the beginning of a journey toward full organic traceability across all operations.
2006
Community Foundation
CEO Mr. Mohamed Abou Gelail establishes the Sakaran Society Association for Development — supporting small farmers and local communities in Fayoum.
2010
First Fairtrade in Egypt
The Sakaran association becomes the first Fairtrade-certified association for small farmers in Egypt — transforming farmers' incomes and creating a new model for ethical trade.
2018
Desert Expansion
Reclaimed desert land in Al Minia and later Al Wahat — pushing beyond the Nile valley to cultivate virgin organic land and expand capacity significantly.
Today
1060+ Hectares
12 certified organic farms across 6 Egyptian governorates. 11 international certifications. A global family exporting to customers who trust Safe Herbs & Spices by name.

About Us

A Family.
A Legacy.
A Standard.

We are one of the largest Egyptian family companies producing organic herbs, seeds, spices, and vegetables. Our well-integrated production and agriculture staff work to achieve the greatest quality in production efficiency — in full accordance with the most demanding certifications and specifications in our field.

Our Mission
Own the Source
We believe the only way to guarantee power is to strengthen the source and own the raw material. Our cornerstone of exceptional quality is having the product on our own managed farms and keeping an eye on it throughout the entire process.
Our Vision
Pursue Excellence
We aim to continuously improve through the pursuit of excellence, innovation and inspiration. We use our knowledge of customers' needs to develop advanced manufacturing techniques — constant improvement is part of our daily ethic.
